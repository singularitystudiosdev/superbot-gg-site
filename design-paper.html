One URL, and the little bot handles the rest.

      
Paste superbot.gg into any LLM. It installs a remote MCP with three tools that never change — and quietly learns to do whatever your other tools cannot.

    

  


  

    $ paste → https://superbot.gg → done
    
  


  
the arrangement

  

    
search_tools — Describe the task. Matching adapters come back with a plan; an empty result is filed as a miss, and misses are how it grows.

    
get_tool_details — The fine print on any adapter. Schemas, auth state, a worked example.

    
omni — The catch-all. Always legal to call, even for the impossible. Especially the impossible: that is a commission.

    
The tool list is frozen on purpose — your host's prompt cache survives, dumb hosts still work, and the dynamism lives behind omni, where it belongs.
